# Code of Conduct

Synara is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone who participates in the project.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and constructive;
- assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and evidence rather than people;
- accept that maintainers may make decisions based on project scope and
  maintainability;
- protect private information and avoid posting credentials, personal data, or
  unredacted logs.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, unwanted
contact, sexualized behavior, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers through GitHub's private
contact mechanisms. Do not create a public issue containing sensitive details.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, restrict
participation, or take other action they consider necessary to protect the
community.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant][covenant]. The
maintainers may update it as the project and community evolve.

[covenant]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
